Tikku Run - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

What the game stores on your device

Your progress, scores, settings, unlocked characters and levels you build are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device.

What is sent online

  • Online leaderboard (optional): when you finish a run, the name you type and your score are sent to our leaderboard (Google Firebase) so other players can see them. Don't use your real full name if you don't want it shown.
  • Cloud save (optional): only if you press "Upload", your game progress is stored online under a random code. Anyone with that code can load it, so keep it private.
  • Visitor counting: we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that does not use cookies and does not track you across websites. It counts page views and game events (for example "game started").

What we do not collect

No account, no email, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no ads and no in-app purchases. Gems in the shop are earned only by playing.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the website this game is published on.
